# Research-informed visual craft for SiteFly

Apply to a new direction or an explicitly requested redesign. A scoped fix retains the existing identity. This is SiteFly's adaptation, not installation of upstream packages. Source revisions and repository-level popularity observations are in `data/design-methods/2026-09-29.json`; see [sources](sources.md). Popularity helps select material to study, never proves output quality.

For the current all-page execution and evidence method, read [visual completion](visual-completion.md). It applies to the authorized scope in every mode: purposeful space, whole-page hierarchy, color relationships, independent PC/mobile composition and brand coherence. Familiar shopping layouts may be finely designed; novelty and decoration are not acceptance proxies.

## Make the borrowed method visible in the result

Use the existing `site-design.json` and author self-check records, not another approval form or required new schema. During reference study, record a short chain: **observed strength → shopper benefit → original adaptation → actual page evidence**. A reference URL, named skill or adjectives such as “premium” do not fill that chain. Apply the most relevant methods; do not mechanically exercise every source on every edit.

- **Editorial selection:** choose an honest shopping reason to open the page and a useful next step. The first two folds should select and arrange actual merchants/offers, not present every record with equal visual weight. No invented sale, popularity, redemption guarantee or decorative explanation to manufacture appeal.
- **Art direction:** plan the promotional visual's subject, medium, light/material, palette relationship, desktop composition and phone crop together. Text and real controls remain HTML. Produce or obtain suitable authorized art before settling the page; a generic shopping bag, an unrelated photograph or a large empty shape does not become good artwork because it is original.
- **Coherent expression:** decide type roles, hierarchy, base/accent relationships, spaces and genuine action anatomy as one system. Within a site, reuse decisions coherently. Across new sites, allow different silhouettes, groupings, densities and button treatments where they fit. Do not solve cross-site sameness by imposing a different universal shape.
- **Content rhythm:** make the second fold earn another scroll through useful contrast in emphasis and browsing purpose. Cards, open rows, grouped logos and editorial surfaces are available choices, not a required sequence. Keep necessary data easy to scan; visual variety must not turn coupon shopping into decoding a metaphor.
- **Inner-page transfer:** preserve the explicit business framework while expressing this identity in store information, offer rows, code/action regions and recommendations. Inspect a representative detail at realistic long-title and narrow-width states before multiplying the renderer. A home-only skin is insufficient.
- **Finishing during production:** inspect actual desktop and phone first two folds, image crop, font loading, text wrapping, gaps, real button states and redraws. Correct related causes together before handoff. Keep the larger complete review sweep and repair packet in the existing workflow; this adds neither a third reviewer nor extra serial polish rounds.

## How the independent reviewer judges value

First look without the author's rationale, then compare the real candidate with the supplied excellent references at matched viewports and relevant regions. Record concrete strengths and gaps across the existing six benchmark dimensions: hero, first two folds, typography, imagery, cards/actions and mobile. Use the existing coverage/benchmark/defect fields; do not add a second scorecard. Evidence must describe both the candidate and the reference, and explain the shopper-facing difference. A subjective verdict still needs an observable basis.

For example, “the promotional object and offer compete at 390px, the action falls below an empty image area, while the reference preserves a clear offer/action group” is actionable. “Needs more wow” is not. Preserve successful choices; request outcomes and locations rather than a replacement theme. Separate a major problem from an optional taste preference. Missing evidence remains not-run. Originality, passing dimensions, many pages or a novel illustration cannot offset an unattractive, confusing or unfinished result.

After a repair, verify the whole closed packet and affected regressions on the new candidate. Keep reusable causes in production lessons, while the brand-specific solution stays in that site's plan. Final acceptance still requires the business, fact, responsive and extracted ZIP checks. Improving this method is not evidence that a new UI has passed it.
